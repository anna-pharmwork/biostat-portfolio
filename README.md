## 📊 Projects

### 1. Survival Analysis in the PBC Trial

> A full clinical-biostatistics analysis of a **randomized, placebo-controlled trial** in Primary Biliary Cirrhosis (n=312), evaluating D-penicillamine vs. placebo.

🔗 **[View the full HTML report →](./pbc_survival_analysis.html)**  
📁 **Source code:** [`pbc_survival_analysis.Rmd`](./pbc_survival_analysis.Rmd)

**Methods applied:**
- ✅ Baseline characteristics table (Table 1) with standardized mean differences
- ✅ Kaplan-Meier survival curves with log-rank test
- ✅ Multivariable **Cox Proportional Hazards** regression
- ✅ Proportional hazards assumption testing (`cox.zph`)
- ✅ Logistic regression as secondary confirmatory analysis

**Key findings:**
- D-penicillamine showed **no survival benefit** over placebo (HR = 1.19, 95% CI [0.82–1.71], p = 0.365)
- **Log-bilirubin** emerged as the strongest independent predictor of mortality (HR = 2.60, p < 0.001)
- Albumin, age, and disease stage were significant prognostic covariates

**Skills demonstrated:** clinical trial interpretation · covariate selection · assumption checking · reproducible reporting
